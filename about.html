RebootMonitor for Android

Keeping track of Android device reboots sounds simple, but in many organizations it can be surprisingly difficult.

Shared devices, warehouse scanners, kiosk systems, reception tablets, and frontline worker devices are often used by multiple employees throughout the day. Unlike personal smartphones, these devices usually have no dedicated owner. As users change shifts or hand devices over to colleagues, information about when a device was last restarted is quickly lost.

As a result, devices can remain operational for weeks or even months without a reboot.

Why Regular Reboots Matter

While Android devices are designed for continuous operation, regular reboots remain an important part of device maintenance.

Restarting a device can:

  • Complete pending operating system and security updates
  • Free system memory and resources
  • Improve device performance
  • Resolve temporary application issues
  • Restore system stability
  • Reduce unexpected disruptions
  • Help business-critical applications run reliably

When troubleshooting device issues, one of the first questions asked by support teams is often:

“When was the device last restarted?”

Unfortunately, the answer is frequently unknown.

Meet RebootMonitor

RebootMonitor is a lightweight Android application that provides immediate visibility into device uptime and reboot information.

Instead of navigating through Android settings or guessing when a device was last restarted, RebootMonitor displays the information directly on the device in a clear and easy-to-understand format.

Whether you're an IT administrator, support engineer, or end user, RebootMonitor helps you quickly determine the current status of a device.

Key Features

  • Shows the exact date and time of the last reboot
  • Displays current device uptime
  • Verifies that a requested reboot has actually taken place
  • Helps identify devices that have been running for long periods
  • Supports troubleshooting and maintenance activities
  • Helps confirm that updates requiring a reboot have been completed
  • Optional reboot reminders after a configurable uptime period
  • Ability to disable reminders when not required
  • Lightweight and easy to use
  • No complicated setup required

RebootMonitor does more than simply display reboot information. By making uptime and reboot history visible, it helps users become more aware of the importance of regular device restarts. Over time, this encourages healthier reboot routines, helping devices remain stable, responsive, and up to date. Small habits such as regular reboots can have a significant impact on device reliability and overall user experience.
