Does Android keep a reboot history?

What Android records about restarts, what it does not, and how to keep a history of your own.

The short answer: not in a form you can use

Android has no built-in screen that lists your earlier restarts.

Some phones can show how long they have been running since the last start, but that number resets with every restart and the earlier ones are gone. Technical logs exist for developers, but they are short-lived and not meant for everyday use.

That makes it hard to answer simple questions: When did this phone last restart? How often does it restart? Did that restart I asked for actually happen?

Does Android keep a reboot history?
